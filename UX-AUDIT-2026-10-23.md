## The real problem

**Two problems, both visual:**
1. **Understandability.** Many sections do not say what they are or what to do. Examples: "the people behind the pins." under photo bubbles, the "what's new?" side rails with sideways words, the tile grid on community, the mono-caps labels everywhere. A first-time visitor has to decode each block.
2. **Visual languages attack each other.** One scroll of the home page has: a pixel-halftone globe, glossy Blender toys (mountain, globe + paper plane, mole on a cake), a CSS podium, a duotone photo with an SVG seal, a photo-bubble collage, a looping video, photo cards in three templates, and solid blue bars. Each is fine alone. Next to each other they fight, and nothing reads as "DeepSpot".

The fix is one visual system (the tours page recipe) plus one big graphic per section. The rest of this doc is that.

## 1. One visual system: "the mole world" (Charlie 10/23: the tours page is the reference)

The tours page is the best-looking page on the site and the one that feels most like DeepSpot. Make its recipe the rule
for every graphic.

### What makes the tours page work (the recipe)
- **A word is the set.** The page's word ("tours") is built as big 3D letters (Arial Bold extruded, flat cream shading: front 1.0 / sides .80 / top .92).
- **Moles work on the word.** Construction moles (hard hat, hi-vis vest, tools) climb, carry, hammer, take a smoke break. They give the word a story.
- **Front-on orthographic camera**, on the site's dark navy. No perspective tricks, no glossy studio light.
- **Slow held-pose loop**: each mole cycles 5 poses, 12 frames each. Charming, not busy.
- **One loud brand prop** (the green tape) and nothing else.
- Recipe in code: `Downloads/Mole Export/_construction/tours_scene.py`, mole `BLOOD1_construction.blend`, teaser plan `teaser/PLAN.md`.

### Rule 1 · Three kinds of picture, each with one job
- **The mole world = every DeepSpot graphic.** Section art, banners, feature art, empty states, the 404, loading. Always the tours recipe: the section's word or number in cream 3D letters + moles doing that section's job.
- **The halftone globe = "where".** Only for location: the home hero, the spot page mini-globe, the profile map, later the map page. Not used as decoration.
- **Photos = real places.** Clean rectangles, full-bleed or one clean frame. No collages, bubbles, duotone or stickers.

### Rule 2 · What retires
Glossy toy renders without the tours look (roadmap mountain, globe + paper plane, podium blocks, the cake base), the CSS
podium, the duotone W5 card, the photo-bubble collage, the SVG seals. Each gets a mole-world replacement (table in §2).

### Rule 3 · Four inks only
Navy `#0b1a2e`, cobalt `#3c60ff`, green `#07d653`, cream `#f5f4ee` (the letters). The mole's own colours (hat yellow,
vest orange, grey fur) are the only extra colours, and only on moles. Cyan/teal, lime, pink, purple and olive go.

### Rule 4 · One type system
- Headings: heavy lowercase cream, at most one green word. The two-tone blue/green head stays only on the home hero.
- When a section has mole art, the art IS the heading (the 3D word); the HTML heading stays for screen readers and search.
- Body: sans 17 px+, sentence case. Mono caps only on the passport and coordinates.

### Rule 5 · One shape set
Cards 14 px corners, 1 px line, no shadow. Pill buttons. No solid full-width colour bars as headers.

### Rule 6 · Every section says what it is in one line
Heading = what it is. One line = what you can do. One button with a familiar verb ("see all", "join", "vote", "save").

### Rule 7 · Cost control
Mole art is a Blender render per section. Keep it cheap: one shared scene kit with the letter builder + mole poses
(exists for tours/teaser); each section = a new word + a new prop + new poses. Ship as animated WebP/WebM loops
(<= 400 KB) with a still for reduced motion.

## 4. Simple but unique

- **Familiar structure, unique moments.** Nav, tabs, cards, search, save and share work like Airbnb or Instagram. The surprise is in the feedback (stamps, shakes, tilts, deals) and in the moles, never in how you navigate.
- **One unique thing per screen.** The §2 table is that rule applied.
- **The moles are the signature.** If every DeepSpot graphic is a word built by moles, a screenshot of any page is recognisable as DeepSpot. That is "a specific style".
- **The passport is the reward.** Save a spot → a stamp lands on your passport. Build it after the backend is connected (it needs real saves).
- **Moles react.** Small loops that answer the user (a mole cheers when you save, waves when you join the race) are cheap once the scene kit exists.
