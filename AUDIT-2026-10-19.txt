FINDINGS (most severe first)

HIGH

H1. Page weight. Images are served at camera size to every device.
    - Home: 15.3 MB, 57 requests, same on phone and desktop.
      editorial-assets/boundary.jpg 5.3 MB (4160x3120), spot-south-london-...jpg 2.5 MB,
      site/assets/story-moscow.jpg 1.3 MB.
    - /story/?id=boundary: 28.1 MB. Two Wikimedia originals are hotlinked (7.0 MB + 5.3 MB).
    - /stories/: 8.7 MB. /browse/: 6.6 MB. /roadmap/: 4.1 MB (roadmap-mountain.png 3.5 MB, 2990x1919).
    Fix: make 640/1280/1920 WebP sizes with srcset; copy hotlinked Wikimedia images locally at
    display size (keep the credit); convert roadmap-mountain.png to WebP.

H2. The deploy folder holds dead files.
    - 98 unreferenced files, 12.5 MB: page/index chunks v25–v50, japan.jpg, london.jpg,
      austria.jpg, athens.webp, bridge-lab*.css/js and more.
    - source/backups/ (34 files) is inside the served folder. A deploy of source/ publishes it.
    Fix: move source/backups/ to backups/; move dead files to _archive/ outside source/ before deploy.

H3. Production is 16 days behind. Live = 10/3 (browse). Not live: 189 new and 13 changed files
    (community hub, moles, promo banners, passport round 1, design and journey audits).

MEDIUM

M1. Small text on cobalt #3c60ff fails 4.5:1 on 16 pages.
    - Cream #f5f4ee on cobalt = 4.42. Affects .ds-cta-contribute p (13 pages), membership,
      competition prize panel.
    - Tinted text is lower: #e1e5ff 3.91 (.ds-wide-cta p), #c9d9ff 3.44 (eyebrows),
      community slide copy 3.97–4.36, top-explorer handle 3.89.
    Fix (pick one): text panels use cobalt #3858f0 (cream 4.99, white 5.49); or set the small text
    to pure #fff (4.87) and stop using blue tints for text on cobalt.

M2. Light-mode home headline: "Un-Ordinary" green #16e05a on cream #f6f3ec = 1.59:1
    (large-text minimum is 3:1). Readable at 140px but weak. Option: a darker green in light mode only.

M3. Module duplicated by cache key. profile/data.mjs imports ../site/account-state.mjs with no
    ?v, while sign-pad.mjs and account.mjs use ?v=pad1. The profile page loads two copies. After a
    deploy, a cached old copy can drop signatureArt in normalizeAccount, so the drawing would not
    show on the passport. Fix: add ?v=pad1 to the data.mjs import.
    Also: site-cta.css is ?v=expansion1 on 26 pages and ?v=footer1 on home, browse and profile.

M4. Home ignores the saved theme. Sub-pages read localStorage 'deepspot-theme'; the home page
    reads only ?theme=. A bookmark or typed URL to / always opens dark. In-site links carry
    ?theme=, so normal navigation works.

M5. No 404 page. source/404.html does not exist; a bad URL on Netlify shows the default page.

M6. Leaderboard at 390px: the table (480px) scrolls sideways inside a 356px box. The
    "points" column is off-screen with no scroll cue.

LOW

L1. Accessibility structure (axe, all views):
    - The top ticker (.br-ticker) sits outside any landmark on 30 pages.
    - Heading order skips on /spot/, /stories/, /guides/, /app/, /cookies/.
    - <aside> nested inside another landmark on /spot/, /inbox/, /community/, /cookies/.
    - role="group" on <article> carousel slides (home what's-new, community hub).
L2. Tap targets under 24px at 390: /contribute/ 29, /stories/ 25, /spot/ 24, /story/ 22.
L3. Every route downloads featured-spots.json (233 KB) and site/content.json (119 KB), also
    pages that do not use them (/account/, /competition/).
L4. Legal pages: "01.What Are Cookies?", "01.Information We Collect" — no space after the number
    (copied from the source text in site/content.json).
L5. No og:title / og:image / twitter tags on any page. Shared links have no preview card.
L6. /promo-variations.html (review page only): 509px wide at 390 (horizontal scroll);
    loads promo-variations.js with no ?v.
